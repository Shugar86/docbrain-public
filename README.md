# DocBrain complaint letters and public examples

[DocBrain](https://docbrain.online/us) helps you turn an everyday problem into a clear complaint or demand letter. Describe what happened and what you want fixed. The US service costs **$4.99 once** and includes a PDF, an editable Word document and sending instructions. There is no subscription.

This is a public introduction maintained by the **DocBrain team**, not an independent review or a collection of customer testimonials. The application source code and all customer information remain private.

## What you can use it for

- A landlord has not returned your security deposit.
- An employer has not paid wages you earned.
- A company keeps charging after you canceled.
- A purchase never arrived, a refund is missing, or a contractor left work unfinished.

You describe the situation rather than choosing a legal document type. Include your state when it matters, along with dates, amounts and the result you want. The document organizes those facts into a request, a response deadline and a next step.

## How an order works

1. Choose your country and describe the problem in your own words.
2. Enter your delivery email and pay the displayed one-time price through Stripe Checkout.
3. Download your PDF and Word files from the private order page. An email provides the delivery notification. Review names, dates, amounts and any placeholders before sending the letter yourself.

DocBrain prepares the document; it does not send a demand to the other party, file a case, represent you or guarantee payment. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.
